        Pollution Source Audit & Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP)

        Multi-agency emergency mitigation measures mandated for Greater Chennai Corporation.

        STAGE I • POOR

        AQI 201 – 300

        • Strict enforcement of ban on open garbage burning across landfills (Kodungaiyur & Perungudi).
        • Mechanized sweeping and water sprinkling on arterial roads (GST Road, Inner Ring Road, OMR).
        • Strict pollution-under-control (PUC) checks for heavy diesel commercial vehicles.
        STAGE II • VERY POOR

        AQI 301 – 400

        • Ban on usage of diesel generator (DG) sets across commercial malls and corporate IT parks.
        • Ennore & Manali industrial units ordered to curb production capacity by 20%.
        • Enhanced frequency of Chennai Metro rail and MTC electric bus fleets to reduce private vehicle transit.
        STAGE III • SEVERE

        AQI 401 – 450

        • Immediate halt on all construction and demolition (C&D) operations across Greater Chennai.
        • Strict ban on entry of non-essential diesel container trucks into Chennai Port through city limits.
        • Closure of stone crushers, brick kilns, and hot mix plants in Chennai Metropolitan Area.
        STAGE IV • SEVERE+

        AQI > 450 (EMERGENCY)

        • Mandatory work-from-home (WFH) for 50% staff across government and private offices.
        • Physical school closure for primary and middle school students; shift to online classes.
        • Consideration of odd-even vehicular rationing on major arterial roads.
